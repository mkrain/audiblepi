AudiblePi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

AudiblePi is a music player that turns the digits of pi into notes. This page explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

What AudiblePi collects

AudiblePi itself collects nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It runs no analytics of its own and sends no data to the developer. Your settings and playback position are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Advertising is the one exception, and it is handled by Google, as described next.

Advertising

AudiblePi shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Device information such as model and operating system version
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions and taps

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer of AudiblePi. Google's handling of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in AdMob's documentation.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings, under Privacy, then Ads. The same screen lets you turn off ad personalization. Deleting the advertising ID does not affect the app itself.

All ages

AudiblePi is a general-audience app. It is not designed specifically for children, but nothing in it excludes anyone of any age, and the app itself collects no data from any user, young or old. The only data handling in the app comes from the ads, which are supplied by Google AdMob and follow Google's policies. Ad personalization can be turned off for any user in Android settings, under Privacy, then Ads.
